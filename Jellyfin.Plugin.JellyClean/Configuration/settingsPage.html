Safeguards

What is never removed, by hand or from the Leaving soon list. A protected item also keeps the season and series it is in. Items being played right now are always kept. The preview shows what stays and why.

0 or empty switches a number or the tag off. Add the tag to an item in Jellyfin (Edit metadata, Tags).

Never remove anything from these libraries

Leaving soon

Put items on the Leaving soon list from the preview on the Library page, instead of removing them right away. They are due after the notice period, and then wait for you to review and remove them. Safeguards are checked again at removal, so an item someone started watching in the meantime stays.

Saved views

Save a view on the Library page. A view checked daily puts its new matches on the Leaving soon list; it never removes anything itself.

Radarr and Sonarr

Without them, a deleted movie or episode is seen as missing and downloaded again. With them, JellyClean removes the movie from Radarr, removes the series from Sonarr when you remove a whole series, and stops monitoring the episodes when you remove seasons or episodes. If Radarr or Sonarr can't be reached, the files stay. The Jellyfin server connects to them, not your browser, so use the address that works on the server, like http://192.168.1.10:8989, even when you manage Jellyfin from away. Add a second Radarr or Sonarr, like a 4K one, if you have it. Rather not add API keys? Switch on Unmonitor Deleted Movies (Radarr) and Unmonitor Deleted Episodes (Sonarr) under Settings, Media Management instead: they then stop monitoring what disappears from disk, though the movie or series stays listed there.
