Protection

Items marked keep on the Library page are never removed, and neither is the season or series they are in. Items being played are never removed either.

Radarr and Sonarr

Without them, a deleted movie or episode is seen as missing and downloaded again. With them, JellyClean removes the movie from Radarr, removes the series from Sonarr when you remove a whole series, and stops monitoring the episodes when you remove seasons or episodes. If Radarr or Sonarr can't be reached, the files stay. Add a second Radarr or Sonarr, like a 4K one, if you have it.
